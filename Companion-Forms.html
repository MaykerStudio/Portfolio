Form Identity & Gameplay Mutation

Each form was designed around a unique gameplay philosophy, combat identity, motion language, and modular gameplay mutations. The module system already existed in the project and was integrated into the AI Tool framework, allowing for flexible and reusable gameplay logic across forms.

01

Wrath

The wrath form focuses on aggressive and damage, turning the companion into a powerful turret that shoots projectiles on enemies around. The enemy detection and projectile management systems were designed using the 2D AI Tool framework, allowing for modular and reusable gameplay logic across forms.

Module 01

The 6-shooter module increases the companions's capacity and rapid-fire adds quick burst of projectiles

Burning Wrath

The Burning Wrath module makes Dirge burn nearby enemies every second.

Rapid Fire & Explosive Payload

Separate modules recorded together: Rapid Fire greatly increases the shooting fire rate, and Explosive Payload makes bullets explode.

02

Envy

Envy turns projectile enemies against themselves: Dirge grabs every incoming shot fired at the player, stores it, and fires it back at the nearest enemy with its original damage and behavior. The form was designed around modular gameplay mutations, letting modules expand the arsenal, duplicate shots, convert stored projectiles into faster cooldowns, or endlessly recycle caught shots.

Reverse Engineering & Supply Chain

Separate active modules recorded together: Reverse Engineering recycles the last caught shot back into the queue, and Supply Chain lets Dirge collect Shiro's projectiles too.

Conversion, Duplication & Expanded Arsenal

Separate passive modules recorded together: Conversion refunds the cooldown with stored projectiles, Duplication adds a chance to fire a bonus copy, and Expanded Arsenal increases how many projectiles Dirge can hold.

03

Lust

Dirge's Lust form launches slow, drifting projectiles that meander around the battlefield and lazily curve toward enemies, charming them on contact. Charmed foes turn on their allies for a short time, and modular mutations can add damage-over-time, memento explosions on death, aggro focus, longer projectile uptime, and donations.

Love Bites & Donation

Separate modules recorded together: Love Bites adds damage over time to charmed enemies, and Donation makes charmed enemies bleed currency on an interval.

Memento & With Open Arms

Separate modules recorded together: Memento triggers an explosion when a charmed enemy dies, and With Open Arms increases how long Lust projectiles stay active.

04

Gluttony

Gluttony turns Dirge into a predator: it locks onto the closest enemy, runs it down, and latches on, biting for damage over time every second. Modular mutations let the feast spread to nearby enemies, heal Shiro from the damage dealt, detonate kills in shockwaves, extend the form's duration with every meal, or keep devoured enemies dropping currency.

Eyes Too Big & Leech

Separate modules recorded together: Eyes Too Big turns Dirge's killing blows into shockwave explosions, and Leech heals Shiro for a percentage of the damage Dirge deals.

Not Full Yet, Picky Eater & Seconds Please

Separate modules recorded together: Not Full Yet extends the form's duration with each kill, Picky Eater keeps devoured enemies dropping currency, and Seconds Please spreads the feast to nearby enemies.

05

Greed

Greed turns Dirge into a self-serving predator: it charges enemies on sight and slams into them, forcing their currency loose, then hoards every coin in an orbiting ring around itself. Modular mutations can erupt shockwaves on impact, duplicate collected coins, double all income while sharing the hoard with Shiro, tractor currency from extreme range, or spend the whole hoard firing a sweeping laser beam.

Double Lucky & Gigantomachia

Separate modules recorded together: Double Lucky doubles all currency income and shares the hoard with Shiro, and Gigantomachia makes Dirge's slams erupt in shockwaves.

Hoarder & My Gamble

Separate modules recorded together: Hoarder gives collected coins a chance to duplicate, and My Gamble spends the entire hoard to fire a sweeping laser beam.

06

Sloth

Sloth surrounds Dirge with a ring of orbiting scythes that shred nearby enemies on contact while following her position. The ring spins continuously and retracts when the form ends. Modular mutations add extra scythes, spin them faster, grow them larger, destroy incoming projectiles, or detonate each scythe for area damage on exit.

Bigger is Better & Faster is Better

Separate modules recorded together: Bigger is Better grows the scythes larger, and Faster is Better spins the ring faster.

Silence is Better & The End is Better

Separate modules recorded together: Silence is Better lets the scythes destroy incoming projectiles, and The End is Better detonates each scythe for area damage when the form ends.

07

Pride

Pride turns Dirge into Shiro's personal wall: it floats directly in front of the player and holds an arc shield that blocks enemy projectiles and beams. The shield has its own health pool, and if it breaks the form ends early, so keeping the barrier alive is the whole game. Modular mutations let Dirge detonate shockwaves on entry, burn enemies that touch the shield, set blocked shots on fire, or reinforce the shield with extra hit points.

Explosive Exit & Impenetrable

Separate modules recorded together: Explosive Exit detonates a knockback blast when the form ends and clears nearby projectiles, and Impenetrable reinforces the shield with +50 hit points.

Keep Your Filth & Untouchable

Separate modules recorded together: Keep Your Filth makes blocked shots erupt into a burning flame wave, and Untouchable burns and knocks back any enemy that touches the shield.

Technical Architecture

The system was built around modular gameplay mutation, reusable combat architecture, and scalable technical VFX workflows.

Modular Gameplay Logic

Modules dynamically alter gameplay behavior, attack logic, utility, movement, and combat presentation.

Hybrid VFX Workflow

Combined Unity Particle Systems and VFX Graph for deterministic gameplay timing and layered visual motion.

Readability First

Effects were designed around silhouette preservation, combat clarity, and visual readability during high-intensity gameplay.
